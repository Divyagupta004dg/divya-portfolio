About Me

RTL / VLSI Enthusiast

I am a VLSI and semiconductor enthusiast with a strong interest in RTL design, FPGA-based systems, and the complete RTL-to-GDSII flow. I enjoy working close to hardware, understanding how timing, architecture, and physical constraints shape real silicon.

Currently, I am strengthening my knowledge in static timing analysis, physical design, and verification while building hands-on projects using industry-standard tools like OpenLane, Sky130, and FPGA flows. My long-term goal is to contribute to efficient, scalable, and high-performance digital systems.
